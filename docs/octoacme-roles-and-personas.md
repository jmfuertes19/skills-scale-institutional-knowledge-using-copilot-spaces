# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Professional

### Role Summary
QA / Testing professionals ensure the quality, reliability, and usability of features before release. They validate acceptance criteria and help reduce defects that reach production.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Perform manual QA and exploratory testing for new features
- Collaborate with developers on testability and regression coverage
- Run smoke tests and release validation checks
- Document bugs with clear reproduction steps and severity
- Validate fixes before sign-off

### Goals
- Protect release quality and customer experience
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Improve confidence in delivery readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Acceptance criteria review
- QA sign-off and release readiness discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate fixes and edge cases
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and business impact
- Supports Project Managers by flagging risk and release-readiness gaps

---

## Tech Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, uphold architecture quality, and help the team deliver maintainable solutions. They connect product goals to engineering execution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architectural decisions
- Review code and designs for quality and consistency
- Mentor developers and support problem-solving
- Identify technical risks and escalation paths
- Help estimate work and align delivery with technical constraints

### Goals
- Deliver robust, scalable, and maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and implementation risk
- Improve engineering consistency and team capability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Code reviews and engineering planning
- Technical risk and dependency escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to shape design and implementation quality
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying technical dependencies and delivery constraints

---

## Security Officer / Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure that projects follow secure development practices and meet compliance and risk expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and implementations for security risks
- Define and validate security requirements for workstreams
- Ensure security testing and scanning are included in the process
- Coordinate incident response and remediation activities
- Provide guidance on secure-by-default practices

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and exposure
- Embed security into the delivery lifecycle
- Support safe rollout and compliance

### Typical Communication
- Planning reviews for security requirements
- Security sign-off before release
- Incident triage and remediation coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on secure implementation and review
- Supports Product and Project Managers by surfacing risk and mitigation needs
- Escalates critical security issues through the risk and incident process

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Release Engineers support reliable deployment pipelines, infrastructure stability, and smooth release execution.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage staging and production environments
- Coordinate release windows, rollback plans, and post-deploy checks
- Monitor deployment health and troubleshoot environment issues
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and speed
- Reduce release-related incidents
- Support operational readiness and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness checks
- Pipeline and environment health review
- Incident response and rollback coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to support deployment readiness and observability
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release schedule and rollback plans
- Aligns with Security and Product stakeholders on safe, compliant release execution

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder Lead

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, prioritize business value, and support the project at the decision-making level.

### Responsibilities
- Define business outcomes and strategic goals
- Approve resources, scope trade-offs, and priority decisions
- Review project progress and escalate business blockers
- Support stakeholder alignment and organizational sponsorship

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain executive alignment and support
- Remove cross-functional barriers to execution

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and stakeholder updates
- Escalation of business blockers and strategic decisions
- Go / no-go decisions for major phases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides direction to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Reviews progress with Product Managers and executive stakeholders
- Supports the project through prioritization, risk escalation, and resources
